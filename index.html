Young Suh

I’m a software engineer with 4+ years of experience building and maintaining production applications, automation workflows, data reporting systems, and client-facing solutions. My work has included Angular/.NET development, SQL and MySQL, AWS, production debugging, and translating business requirements into technical implementations. A large part of my work has involved making data and operational workflows easier to use and maintain. For example, I built a reusable SQL reporting solution for versioned SurveyJS data that reduced a report-generation process from potentially hours of manual query modification to roughly a second. I’ve also worked directly with clients on requirements gathering, environment configuration, troubleshooting, and implementation. I’m currently pursuing a Master of Data Science at UC San Diego, where I’m expanding my experience in machine learning, distributed computing, and large-scale data processing. In one Spark project, I worked with roughly 100 GB of seismic waveform data and built an HDF5-to-Parquet conversion pipeline that reduced preprocessing time from about 15 hours to 2–3 hours. I’m most interested in the part of machine learning where data, infrastructure, and modeling come together. I enjoy working with large datasets, building scalable data pipelines, understanding why models behave the way they do, and developing systems that turn data into something useful in production.
